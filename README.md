## 🚀 How to View Your Website

1. Navigate to the `potfolio` folder on your computer.
2. Double-click **`index.html`** to open it in your browser (Chrome, Edge, Firefox, Brave, etc.).

---

## ✏️ How to Add Your Real Photo Later

* Put your photo file (e.g. `my-photo.jpg`) inside the `assets/images/` folder.
* Open `index.html`, find `<img src="assets/images/avatar.svg" alt="M.Varshitha" />` (around line 71) and change it to `<img src="assets/images/my-photo.jpg" alt="M.Varshitha" />`.
